Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how SENBRIX (“we”, “us”) handles information when you visit this website or contact us.

1. Information we receive

  • Details you share with us — such as your name, phone number, email address, material requirements, quantity and delivery location when you call us, message us on WhatsApp, email us, or use the quotation form on this website.
  • Basic technical data — like your IP address, browser type and pages visited, which our hosting provider may record in standard server logs.

2. The quotation form

This website has no database. When you use the quotation form, the details you type are placed into a message that opens in your own WhatsApp or email app. Nothing is sent or stored until you press send in that app, and we do not store the form contents on the website.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiries and prepare quotations.
  • To arrange orders, delivery and billing.
  • To keep records required for our business and by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies of its own and does not use advertising or analytics trackers. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts — the website loads fonts from Google’s servers, which may receive your IP address.
  • Hosting — the website is hosted on Netlify, which may process technical data to deliver the site.
  • WhatsApp and Instagram — if you use these services, their own privacy policies apply.

6. Sharing

We share your information only where needed to fulfil your request (for example with transport partners for delivery), where required by law, or with your consent.

7. Retention and security

We keep enquiry and order records only as long as needed for business, accounting and legal purposes, and take reasonable steps to protect them. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

8. Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to legal and accounting requirements. Contact us using the details below.

9. Children

This website is intended for adults and businesses and is not directed at children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date shows when it was last changed.
