FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our products, bulk orders, quotations and delivery.

What construction materials does SENBRIX supply?

We supply cement (OPC, PPC, PSC and white cement), steel (TMT bars), sand, bricks, concrete blocks, stone aggregates, wall putty, tile adhesive, waterproofing materials, grout, epoxy and jointing mortar.

Does SENBRIX accept bulk orders?

Yes. We support bulk orders for builders, contractors, dealers and developers, with project-based quotations.

How can I get a price quotation?

Tap “Ask for price” on any product, fill in the quotation form, or call us on 93467 82855 or 74162 98474. Share the material, quantity and delivery location and we will reply with a quotation.

Do you deliver to project sites?

We coordinate delivery to project sites, subject to material availability and your location. Please mention your site location when you request a quotation.

Are the prices fixed?

Wholesale prices change with market rates, brand, quantity and location, so we confirm the price in a quotation for every enquiry.

Who do you supply to?

We supply homeowners, builders, contractors, engineers, dealers, developers and construction companies for residential, commercial and industrial projects.
